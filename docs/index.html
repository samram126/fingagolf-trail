Trace a shot

Film with the phone propped up and still. Then pick the clip — you'll tap the ball twice, and it finds the ball in every frame, checks each one up close, and draws the trail.

  • Closer is better: the ball should be easy to see.
  • Higher frame rates (50 or 60 fps) track best.
  • Keep recording until the ball has landed.
